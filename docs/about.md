## Philosophy

### Show the Work

I practice radical transparency. When I make a claim, I show you the code. When I present findings, I share the methodology. Only a genuine expert would risk exposing their raw work to public scrutiny—and that's exactly what I do.

### The 5P Framework

My approach to AI adoption isn't about chasing shiny tools. It's about:

1. **Purpose** — What business problem are you solving?
2. **People** — Who needs training and change management?
3. **Process** — What workflows need redesigning?
4. **Platform** — Which tools fit your needs?
5. **Performance** — How do you measure success?

Technology is the easy part. The hard work is people and process.

### Anti-Hype

I tell audiences when *not* to use AI. If a spreadsheet formula solves your problem faster than a language model, use the spreadsheet. AI isn't magic—it's a tool. Tools have appropriate uses and limitations.

The market is full of consultants who only sell optimism. I sell truth, even when it's uncomfortable.
